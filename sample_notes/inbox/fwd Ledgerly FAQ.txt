Ledgerly Onboarding FAQ

Q: Where is data stored?
A: Customers on the UK plan have all event data stored in London (uk-south). Nothing is replicated outside the UK.

Q: How long does SSO setup take?
A: Typically 1–2 weeks depending on your identity provider.

Q: Is there a contract minimum?
A: 12 months.
